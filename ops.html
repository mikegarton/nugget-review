Sources — priority & economics

One integer per method of acquisition, lower = processed sooner, ties allowed; the queue tie-break is newest-published. Manual adds jump to priority 0 for the first 3 per hour. Priority is editable here with the ops write key. Sources are subscribed or discovered — provenance, not quality; nuggets are rated on merit.

Channel economics — lifetime

Cost = Claude tokens + prorated Supadata credits, from the call log. Nugget counts are surviving nuggets (1★ rows are purged). Disabled channels are campaign discoveries or manual-add hosts, not part of the subscribed poll list. Value rates are per-dollar — nug/$ and 5★/$ — because substack costs run magnitudes below video costs and "nuggets per dollar" compares cleanly where "$0.00003 per nugget" reads as noise; bigger is better, like every density column. Substack transcript/minutes columns are legitimately blank; nug/item and **nug/kw** (nuggets per 1,000 words of transcript or post) are the density metrics both sources share — nug/kw counts only items processed since word counting began (2026-07-30), on both sides of the ratio. Channels processed before 2026-07-25 predate cost logging and show $0 (per-dollar rates blank). Tap a column header to sort.
