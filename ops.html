Campaign yield by query

Prime indicators first (nug/item · nug/$ · nug/kw — cost is every call cost of the videos that query found), then the funnel: found → screened out / promoted → processed → nuggets → your ratings. A query with finds but no ≥4★ after a couple of weeks is a candidate for retirement (update yt_campaign_queries set active = false …).

Sources — priority & economics

One integer per method of acquisition, lower = processed sooner, ties allowed; the queue tie-break is newest-published. Manual adds jump to priority 0 for the first 3 per hour. Priority is editable here with the ops write key. Sources are subscribed or discovered — provenance, not quality; nuggets are rated on merit.

Channel economics — lifetime

Columns: identity, then prime value rates, then quality scores, then volume. Cost = Claude tokens + prorated Supadata credits, from the call log. Nugget counts are surviving nuggets (1★ rows are purged). Disabled channels are campaign discoveries or manual-add hosts, not part of the subscribed poll list. Value rates are per-dollar — nug/$ and 5★/$ — because substack costs run magnitudes below video costs and "nuggets per dollar" compares cleanly where "$0.00003 per nugget" reads as noise; bigger is better, like every density column. nug/mo = surviving nuggets ÷ months since the channel's first processed item (denominator floored at 1 month, like views/day's 1-day floor). taste = the AI's guess at your interest (0–100); mentor = the AI's outside-expert value-per-time score (0–100); pk/med/avg = peak, median, average over surviving nuggets. hype (−5…+5) is per scored video, including zero-nugget ones. Substack transcript/minutes columns are legitimately blank; nug/item and nug/kw (nuggets per 1,000 words of transcript or post) are the density metrics both sources share — nug/kw counts only items processed since word counting began (2026-07-30), on both sides of the ratio. Channels processed before 2026-07-25 predate cost logging and show $0 (per-dollar rates blank). Tap a column header to sort.
